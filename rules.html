The Rules

← Back to home

The LoveAll Futsal Tournament follows standard football / futsal rules with a few specific additions to keep matches fast and fair. All participants are expected to play in the spirit of the game.

01

General Rules

⚽

Standard Football Rules Apply

All standard football / futsal rules are in effect — fouls, free kicks, corner kicks, goal kicks — unless otherwise specified below.

👕

5-a-Side Format

Each team fields 5 players on the pitch at a time, with 1 substitute allowed per team. Substitutions can be made during stoppages in play.

🤝

Fair Play

Dangerous or reckless challenges are prohibited. Repeated foul play may result in a player being asked to leave the pitch.

🚫

No Slide Tackles

Slide tackles are strictly not allowed. Violation results in a free kick to the opposing team. Repeated violations may result in a yellow or red card.

⏱️

6 Minutes Per Half

Each match consists of two halves of 6 minutes each. The clock runs continuously.

☕

5-Minute Half-Time Break

Teams receive a 5-minute break between halves to regroup, hydrate, and strategise.

⚡

Extra Time (if needed)

If a knockout match is level at full time, up to 2 minutes of extra time will be played. If still level, a penalty shootout will decide the winner.

03

Referee & Officials

🟡

Decision of the Referee is Final

The referee's decision on all matters — including fouls, goals, timing, and player conduct — is final and binding. Arguments with the referee will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action, including removal from the tournament.

📋

Full Authority

The referee has full authority to manage the match from the moment they enter the pitch until they leave after the final whistle.

🙏

Respect All

All players, substitutes, and team officials must respect the referee and opponents at all times. Unsporting behaviour will be penalised accordingly.

04

Tournament Structure

🏆

Group Stage

Teams play round-robin within their group. Points: Win = 3, Draw = 1, Loss = 0. Top teams advance to the knockout rounds.

⚔️

Knockout Rounds

Single-elimination format — no second chances. Extra time and penalties apply as described above.

🥇

The Final

The top two teams face off for the LoveAll Futsal Trophy. If level after normal time, extra time and penalties decide the champion.
